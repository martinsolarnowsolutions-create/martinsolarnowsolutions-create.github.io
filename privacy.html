Privacy Policy

Hermey  ·  last updated 8 November 2026

Hermey is a private, single-user application. It is operated by one person for their own use and has no other users, no sign-up and no hosted service. It does not collect information from the public.

Information the application accesses

Hermey requests access to Google Sheets through the Google Sheets API, using the spreadsheets scope. It uses this to read and update spreadsheets belonging to the account holder who authorises it.

The application does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive file listings, Contacts, Calendar, or any other Google service.

How that information is used

  • To read existing rows from the account holder's own spreadsheets
  • To add and update rows in those same spreadsheets
  • To prepare summaries of their contents for the account holder's own reference

Spreadsheet contents are processed on the account holder's own computer and are used for no purpose other than maintaining those spreadsheets.

Sharing

No information accessed through the Google Sheets API is sold, rented, published, or shared with any third party. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning models. It is not transmitted to any server operated by the developer, because there is no such server.

Storage and retention

Authorisation credentials are stored locally on the account holder's own computer, readable only by that user account. Working copies of spreadsheet data are held locally for the purpose of preparing updates, and are retained only as long as needed for that purpose. Nothing is stored off that machine.

Withdrawing access

The account holder can revoke the application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops all further reading and updating of spreadsheets.

Children

The application is not directed at children and is not available to anyone other than its single operator.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue on GitHub.
