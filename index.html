Hermey

A private assistant for property market tracking

Hermey is a private, single-user tool that helps maintain property market tracking spreadsheets for residential real estate in New South Wales, Australia. It collects publicly available property listing information, organises it, and keeps the owner's own Google Sheets up to date.

This is not a public service. Hermey is operated by and for one person, on one computer. There is no sign-up, no hosted service, and no other users.

What it does

  • Records residential property listings, price guides, sale results and withdrawals
  • Maintains tracking spreadsheets owned by the operator in Google Sheets
  • Produces summaries of market activity for the operator's own reference

What it connects to

Hermey uses the Google Sheets API to read and update spreadsheets belonging to the operator's own Google account. It is granted access to specific spreadsheets by that account holder and is used for no other purpose.
